If we look at "negative societal reputation", the laTaka-melaka prahasanam, comissioned under the 12th century Gahadavala Govindachandra, portrays the ekadandin Kevaladvaitin Phunkatamishra as associated with nastika-s (Buddhists, Jains) and socially repulsive groups (Kaula-s, Kapalika-s, prostitutes, false Ayurvedic practitioners) all as viśvāsaghātī.
