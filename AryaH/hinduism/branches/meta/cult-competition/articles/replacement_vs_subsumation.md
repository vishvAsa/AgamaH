None of them have been replaced, they’re worshipped as part of daily rites and certain select festivals and auspicious days, at times these Lords are also venerated within the cultic worship of other specific sectarian traditions in a complementary/supplementary fashion.

What you describe as a "degeneration" is moreso the success of sectarian expansionism, imagine if the Dodekatheon and their individual cults to each Olympian were subsumed into or de-popularised by the now expanding cults to say, Dionysus, Artemis and Zeus. And various hero-cults for apotheosised Hemitheoi and ancestors were recognised as emanations/incarnations of these three according their followers and had this elevated to being as important as the worship of those three.

And if each follower of each deity argued that their patron was truly the Transcendent One from which all other beings and deities (including the other two) manifested from. Of which some subsects argue that the apotheosised heroes were in-fact the actual Absolute and not merely incarnations of Zeus or Dionysus. This is a crude and somewhat shabby, but close enough, analogue to Hinduism.
