mogol tyrant Akbar watches the tamAshA as he has the astra-dhArin pUrI and the giri advaita-vedAnta-saMnyAsin-s fight each other at sthAneshvara. In April 1567, when a dispute broke out between the saMnyAsin sects over priority of access during a solar eclipse, the mogol suggested that they settle it using their arms. giri-s were gaining the upper hand when Akbar decided to balance it out by sending his imperial guard to fight on the side of the pUrI-s. As a result, the pUrI-s won and killed Ananda-giri, the mahAnta of the giri-s, even as Akbar had his entertainment.
