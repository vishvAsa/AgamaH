A few e.g.s of paradigm shifting finds:

1. A new veda shAkhA with a notable fraction of new brAhmaNa or mantra material.
2. Archaic medical text replete with material like to older parts of the kAshyapa saMhitA (e.g., its skandopAkhyAna).
3. A new original yAmala or bhairava or bhUta tantra
4. A mathematical text with an algorithm of the order of the kuTTaka or even the chakravAla.
5. An early astronomical text delving deeply into non-nakShatra constellations -- this would paradigm shattering.
6. Pre-AryabhaTTa mathematical texts with the trigonometric ratio of quadratic or other approximations.
7. A physics beyond that of prashastapAda.
8. Detailed zoological treatise.
