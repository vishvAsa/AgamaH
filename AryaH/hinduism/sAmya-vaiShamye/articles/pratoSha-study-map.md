## Base (प्रमाण)
### संस्कृत · Sanskrit
- the foundational language of all these traditions

### Vyākaraṇa · grammar** [4-6 yr]
- Aṣṭādhyāyī · Mahābhāṣya · Siddhāntakaumudī · Laghuśabdenduśekhara

### Pramāṇa Śāstra
- studied in its own right — every tradition writes one
- Tattvacintāmaṇi (Nyāya) · Mānameyodaya (Mīmāṃsā) · Vedāntaparibhāṣā (Advaita)
- Pramāṇapaddhati (Dvaita) · Nyāyapariśuddhi (Viśiṣṭādvaita) · Pramāṇavārttika (Bauddha) · Pramāṇamīmāṃsā (Jaina)

### Navya-Nyāya
- pramāṇa śāstra at its most technical · enter by Tarkasaṅgraha, ~1 yr
- the idiom everything above is written in, after c. 1325

### **Gaṇita · Jyotiṣa** · c. 500 - 1700 CE
- **number · magnitude**
- Siddhānta · gaṇita · the Kerala school · Āryabhaṭīya
- Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta · Siddhāntaśiromaṇi · Yuktibhāṣā · Kerala

### Mīmāṃsā · c. 200 BCE - 1700 CE
- **dharma · apūrva**
- Bhāṭṭa · Prābhākara · Arthasaṅgraha

## PRAMEYA 
WHAT EACH SAYS THERE IS

### nāstika

#### Cārvāka · c. 600 BCE - 800 CE
- **four bhūtas**
- no text survives — pūrvapakṣa only
- source: **Bṛhaspatisūtra** — lost entirely, known by quotation

#### Bauddha · c. 150 - 1100 CE
- **momentary dharmas**
- Madhyamaka · Yogācāra · Pramāṇa
- key texts: Mūlamadhyamakakārikā · Abhidharmakośa · Tattvasaṅgraha

#### Jaina · c. 200 - 1700 CE
- **jīva · ajīva**
- Anekāntavāda · Syādvāda · Tattvārthasūtra
- key texts: Āptamīmāṃsā · Syādvādamañjarī · Parīkṣāmukha

### āstika

#### Sāṅkhya · Yoga · c. 200 - 900 CE
- **puruṣa · prakṛti**
- Satkāryavāda · Sāṅkhyakārikā · Pātañjala Yogasūtra

#### Nyāya · Vaiśeṣika · c. 100 - 1800 CE
- **seven padārthas**
- Prācīna → Navya · 7 padārthas · Nyāyasūtra

#### **Vedānta** · c. 450 - 1700 CE
- **brahman**
- **Brahmasūtra**
- Advaita · Viśiṣṭādvaita · Dvaita

#### Vyākaraṇa · darśana
- Vākyapadīya (Bhartṛhari) · sphoṭa · Vaiyākaraṇabhūṣaṇa · Paramalaghumañjūṣā

### Inter-school Debate Texts [Top]

- Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya — Advaita ≈ Nyāya
- Nyāyāmṛta ≈ Advaitasiddhi — Dvaita ≈ Advaita
- Nyāyamañjarī — Nyāya ≈ Bauddha
- Ślokavārttika — Mīmāṃsā ≈ Bauddha
- Satdūṣaṇī — Viśiṣṭādvaita ≈ Advaita
- Sarvadarśanasaṅgraha — the survey of all of them

### **Sāhitya** · c. 200 BCE - 1700 CE
- **rasa · dhvani**
- Alaṅkāra · Rasa · Dhvani · Kāvyaprakāśa
- Kāvya · Nāṭaka — Raghuvaṃśa · Meghadūta · Śākuntalā · Mṛcchakaṭika
