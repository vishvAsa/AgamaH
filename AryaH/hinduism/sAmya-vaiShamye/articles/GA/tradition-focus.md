## Svabhāva
A firm belief in Svabhāva immensely helps one do dharma without expectation of phala. If it is your svabhāva to do dharma (help Dhārmika causes, spread knowledge, teach as much as your strength allows, etc), do it to the extent you are allowed and you are able to do so. 

## To avoid
Don’t indulge in pointless discourse. It is a huge drain on energy and mental peace.

Those who have a svabhāva for higher things (in various degrees, regardless of their background) will appreciate what you are doing and benefit from your work or even join in (if they have the strength and ability to do so). 

Those who do not have—they will mostly ignore. If they do not and go apeshit, respond once or twice (to defend yourself from slander) and let it go.

All available energy and time should be expended in building communities of common intent and aim—be it virtual or real.

## Proselytization target
Those in a position to “proselytize”— do that internally, aiming at those who have a taste for the higher things but have not had an opportunity to discover their own potential. Don’t waste your time on the hateful who are Hindus only in name.

## Focus
Young people of Sampradāyas should do whatever they can in this regard, in line with their respective abilities.

Every Sampradāya is a potential lifelong anchor for those with the Svabhāva for that Sampradāya. Instead of wasting their lives on “hodgepodge Hinduism” or “Hinduismus Genericus”, they can make the best out of their lives by committing to a codified, rich Sampradāya with a structured path and discipline.
