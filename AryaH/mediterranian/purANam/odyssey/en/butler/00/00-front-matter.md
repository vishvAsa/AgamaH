PREFACE TO SECOND EDITION

BOOK I. THE GODS IN COUNCIL—MINERVA'S VISIT TO ITHACA—THE CHALLENGE FROM TELEMACHUS TO THE SUITORS.

BOOK II. ASSEMBLY OF THE PEOPLE OF ITHACA—SPEECHES OF TELEMACHUS AND OF THE SUITORS—TELEMACHUS MAKES PREPARATIONS AND STARTS FOR PYLOS WITH MINERVA DISGUISED AS MENTOR.

BOOK III. TELEMACHUS AND NESTOR—TELEMACHUS DECLARES HIS ERRAND—NESTOR'S STORY OF THE RETURN OF THE ACHAEANS—NESTOR SENDS TELEMACHUS TO SPARTA.

BOOK IV. TELEMACHUS AND PISISTRATUS AT SPARTA—MENELAUS TELLS TELEMACHUS OF HIS TRAVELS—THE SUITORS CONSPIRE AGAINST TELEMACHUS.

BOOK V. THE GODS IN COUNCIL—CALYPSO IS ORDERED TO RELEASE ULYSSES—HE BUILDS A RAFT AND LEAVES THE ISLAND—HE IS SHIPWRECKED AND REACHES SCHERIA.

BOOK VI. ULYSSES AND NAUSICAA—NAUSICAA TAKES ULYSSES TO THE PALACE OF ALCINOUS.

BOOK VII. ULYSSES IS WELCOMED BY ALCINOUS AND ARETE—HE TELLS HIS STORY.

BOOK VIII. GAMES AND FEASTING—DEMODOCUS SINGS—ULYSSES REVEALS HIMSELF.

BOOK IX. ULYSSES TELLS HIS STORY—THE CICONES—THE LOTUS-EATERS—THE CYCLOPS.

BOOK X. AEOLUS—THE LAESTRYGONIANS—CIRCE.

BOOK XI. THE VISIT TO THE DEAD.

BOOK XII. THE SIRENS—SCYLLA AND CHARYBDIS—THE CATTLE OF THE SUN.

BOOK XIII. ULYSSES REACHES ITHACA—MINERVA ADVISES HIM.

BOOK XIV. ULYSSES AND EUMAEUS.

BOOK XV. TELEMACHUS RETURNS—ULYSSES AND EUMAEUS.

BOOK XVI. ULYSSES REVEALS HIMSELF TO TELEMACHUS.

BOOK XVII. ULYSSES AND TELEMACHUS RETURN TO THE TOWN—ULYSSES IS INSULTED BY MELANTHIUS—HE IS RECOGNISED BY ARGOS.

BOOK XVIII. THE BEGGAR IRUS—ULYSSES FIGHTS HIM—THE SUITORS.

BOOK XIX. ULYSSES AND PENELOPE—THE SCAR.

BOOK XX. OMENS—THE SUITORS FEAST—CTESIPPUS THROWS A OX'S FOOT AT ULYSSES.

BOOK XXI. THE BOW—ULYSSES STRINGS IT—THE SUITORS FAIL.

BOOK XXII. THE DEATH OF THE SUITORS.

BOOK XXIII. ULYSSES AND PENELOPE.

BOOK XXIV. THE GHOSTS OF THE SUITORS IN HADES—ULYSSES AND HIS MEN GO TO THE HOUSE OF LAERTES—THE PEOPLE OF ITHACA COME OUT TO ATTACK ULYSSES, BUT MINERVA CONCLUDES A PEACE.
