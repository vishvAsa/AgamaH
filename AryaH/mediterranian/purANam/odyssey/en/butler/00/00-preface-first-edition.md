---
title: "Preface to First Edition"
---



It has long been a favourite project with me to retranslate the "Odyssey" into English prose, but I was deterred by the knowledge that the ground had been preoccupied by two such excellent translations as those of Mr. S. H. Butcher and Mr. Andrew Lang, and of Mr. W. Leaf. My friend, Mr. Henry Festing Jones, however, urged me to undertake the work, and I accordingly set about it in the autumn of 1899. The translation was finished in May 1900, and the book appeared in July of the same year.

My object has been to give a translation which should be as close as possible to the Greek, without violating the idioms of the English language. I have not attempted to be literal in the strict sense of the word, for a literal translation of Homer is impossible; but I have tried to keep as near to the original as the difference between the two languages will allow. I have not hesitated to sacrifice elegance to accuracy, and I have preferred a bald and sometimes uncouth rendering to one which, though smoother, would have conveyed a different meaning from the Greek.

In the notes I have given my reasons for such departures from the received text as seemed to me to be called for. I have also pointed out various passages which appear to me to be corrupt, and have suggested emendations where they seemed plausible. I have not attempted to give a complete commentary, but only to explain difficulties and to draw attention to points of interest.

The text followed is that of Bekker (1858), except where I have indicated a departure from it. I have also consulted the editions of La Roche (1867-68), Merry (1886), and Monro (1888).

The illustrations are from photographs taken by myself in Sicily and the neighbourhood, except where otherwise stated. They are intended to illustrate the topographical identifications which I have suggested in the notes.

I am indebted to Mr. A. S. Griffith for much valuable assistance in the preparation of this volume, and to Mr. Henry Festing Jones for his encouragement and for reading the proofs.

SAMUEL BUTLER.

120 MAIDA VALE, W.9.\
4th *July*, 1900.
